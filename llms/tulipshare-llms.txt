# Tulipshare

> Tulipshare is a London-based impact investor and shareholder advocacy group that uses activist investing — shareholder proposals, board and ESG-team engagement, and other shareholder rights — to push for ethical and sustainable change at the world's largest companies, on behalf of foundations, asset managers, universities, and pension funds. Backed by Speedinvest. Its website (tulipshare.com) went offline in 2026 and the company publishes no public API surface.
